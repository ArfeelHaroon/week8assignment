My Three Favorite Food Trucks

This is a simple table showcasing my three favorite food trucks in Philadelphia.

Food Truck Information
Food Truck Name Truck Location Truck Photo Food Type Why I Like It
Philly Tacos S. 25th Street & Washington Avenue, South Philadelphia Mexican food, including tacos I like the fresh flavors, and tacos are easy to enjoy as a quick meal.
Pitruco Pizza Changes by day; for example, the truck lists Fairmount at 22nd & Brown on Tuesdays. Made-to-order, wood-fired pizza I like the crispy crust and the variety of toppings available.
The Crepe Truck Philly 1291 W. Norris Street, Philadelphia Sweet and savory crepes I like the versatility of crepes, and they make for a delicious dessert.
